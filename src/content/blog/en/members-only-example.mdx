You are reading this because you are signed in. A visitor who is not sees the
title, the date and the description above — the same words the listing card
carries — with a lock and a way to sign in. The text you are reading now was
never in their response.

## How this post is gated

One line of frontmatter:

```yaml
access: members
```

That is the whole of it. `members` means any signed-in member. Any other value
names a tier from `members.config.ts`, and the member has to hold it:

```yaml
access: pro
```

Nothing else about the post changes. The URL is the one it always had, the
listing still shows it, and the layout is the layout every other post uses.

## What the theme does with it

- **The listing shows a locked card** with the title, date and description, so
  the value is visible before anyone signs in.
- **The post is left out of the RSS feed.** Feeds get republished, and a
  members-only post has no business travelling that way.
- **The post is not prerendered.** It renders on demand so the check can run
  per request, while every public page on the site stays static.
- **With the members area switched off, the post disappears** rather than
  becoming public. A gate that is turned off must not publish what it was
  hiding.

## You will not see this on your own site

This post carries `demoOnly: true`, so it exists only where `MEMBERS_DEMO` is
set — astrorocket.dev, and nowhere else. Enable the members area on your own
site and you get your members and your gated posts, with nothing of the demo
among them and no file to hunt down and delete.
