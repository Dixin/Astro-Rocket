9 Jul – Beyond's guitarist Steve Wong recently went on social media to express his feelings on the 27th anniversary of his brother Wong Ka Kui's passing.

As reported on Mingpao, the singer-songwriter posted a message on Facebook, saying that he couldn't sleep with many thoughts occupying his mind.

"It is unfortunate that I could not walk with you through these changing times, but it feels enough to have had you been by my side. Thank you for your love. Brother, I will always miss you," he wrote.

On the other hand, another member of Beyond, Yip Sai Wing also took to Weibo to write, "We miss you!"

Wong passed away on 30 June 1993, several days after a stage accident that occurred while performing at the Fuji Television studio in Japan. He was 31.
