Three decades after his tragic passing, Abnormal Studios is paying tribute to the beloved Beyond frontman, Wong Ka Kui (黃家駒). “Because of You Ka Kui (真的相識過)” is a cinematic tribute and music event film (大銀幕致敬之作 & 音樂盛事電影) produced by Yip Sai Wing (葉世榮) and Leslie Chan (陳健添), and directed by Rosa Pang (彭瑞祥).

The title was officially launched at the 30th Hong Kong International Film & TV Market (FILMART). More than a film, “Because of You Ka Kui” is a big-screen musical tribute experience dedicated to the life, music, and artistic spirit of Wong Ka Kui, the visionary frontman of Beyond, widely regarded as the most influential Canto rock band of all time.

![](https://hype.my/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/%E7%9C%9F%E7%9A%84%E7%9B%B8%E8%AF%86%E8%BF%87-poster_0306-1-1-1536x2048.webp)

![](https://hype.my/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Poster-2-1-2048x1356.webp)

Decades in the making, this cinematic event brings audiences closer to the man behind the legend through intimate conversations with his closest friends and collaborators. Rather than mythologising an icon, the film offers a deeply human portrait, revealing the ordinary soul whose voice and convictions moved millions.

**A Rare Big-Screen Tribute Experience**

Featuring rare concert footage, never-before-seen archival materials, and unreleased works, this music event film invites audiences to rediscover Wong Ka Kui, not through nostalgia alone, but through the enduring power of his beliefs, artistry, and cultural impact. It is a once-in-a-generation cinematic tribute, designed for the big screen, where music, memory, and emotion converge into a shared experience for fans across generations.

![](https://hype.my/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Poster-3-2048x1402.webp)

**A Legacy That Still Resonates**

More than three decades on, Wong Ka Kui’s music continues to transcend time and geography. Beyond attracts over 1.08 million monthly listeners on Spotify, with “海闊天空 (Boundless Oceans, Vast Skies)” surpassing 72 million streams to date. Across digital platforms, their songs remain deeply embedded in global music culture, proving that Ka Kui’s voice still echoes powerfully today.

**Malaysia’s Enduring Connection**

Abnormal Studios’ connection to the film is also personal to locals. Malaysia remains one of the strongest and most passionate territories for Beyond’s music, with local tribute concerts continuing to draw significant audiences. These events demonstrate a fan base that is not only nostalgic but actively engaged and emotionally connected across generations.

**A Timely Cinematic Event**

The film is scheduled for release in Malaysia in June 2026, building momentum towards Wong Ka Kui’s birthday on 10th June, transforming the release into a meaningful nationwide tribute moment for fans. The film will also roll out across Hong Kong, Macau, Mainland China, and Brunei, with additional territories to be announced soon.
