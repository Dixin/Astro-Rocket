Hong Kong actress-singer Athena Chu tied the knot with Hong Kong singer Paul Wong, member of the band Beyond, in 2012, and welcomed their daughter, Debbie Wong in the same year.  

Recently, netizens posted pictures of the 46-year-old in the airport with her husband. In the pictures, Paul was seen with a huge backpack, as well as a luggage in hand. Meanwhile, Athena, who was dressed in a simple tank top, had a similar luggage in her left hand. Debbie was spotted on her right, and it looked as though the family-of-three was ready to head off for a vacation.  

However, when they reached the gate separating the departure hall from the public area, Athena slowed down, and suddenly bent down to hug Debbie, with tears rolling down her cheeks. A while later, Paul also bent down to gather his wife and daughter into a hug, with the trio clutching each other in tears.

![](https://dam.mediacorp.sg/image/upload/s--ZCKZE5Os--/c_fill,g_auto,h_468,w_830/f_auto,q_auto/v1/8days-migration/zhuyin2-data.jpg?itok=rzxmaJLu)

According to reports, Paul and Debbie were about to head off to film the variety show, *Get Smart! Dad* that day. It was then speculated that Athena could not bear the thought of being apart from her daughter, and let her emotions overwhelm her when they were about to part.  

The actress-singer’s actions have drawn mixed responses from netizens, with some calling her crying fit “too much”, while some sympathised with Athena, saying that it must have been difficult to be apart from her daughter for an extended period of time.
