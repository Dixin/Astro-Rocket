Actress Athena Chu may be 53, but she still brims with youthful energy and effortless charm

HONG KONG: Actress Athena Chu may be 53, but she still brims with youthful energy and effortless charm.

In a recent Weibo post, Chu delighted fans with several snapshots of her outing to the scenic Bolanggu Valley in Shaanxi, China.

Sporting an olive-green tee layered with a light blue jacket and black joggers, she proved that even in laid-back attire, her radiant glow and signature charm remain undeniable.

"Nature is truly incredible," the actress captioned her post.

In the comments, fans showered Chu with praise, noting that she has retained her youthful features and has barely aged since her heyday.

Many also admired how smooth and flawless her skin looked, even with minimal makeup.

"The most beautiful Fairy Zi Xia," one fan commented, referencing Chu's iconic role in the 1995 film 'A Chinese Odyssey'.

Chu made her big-screen debut in the 1992 film 'Fight Back To School II', starring opposite popular filmmaker and actor Stephen Chow.

She is also well-known for her roles in the dramas 'The Legend Of The Condor Heroes' (1994), 'The Boss Up There' (1999), and 'Wall-less World' (2010).

In 2012, Chu married Paul Wong of the Hong Kong rock band Beyond. They have a 13-year-old daughter, Debbie.
