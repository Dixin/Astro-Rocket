'Beyond' puts on farewell concerts

 (Agencies)
Updated: 2005-02-02 15:27


Hong Kong soul rock band "Beyond" comprising of (L-R) lead rocker Steve Wong, drummer Wing Yip and bassist Paul Wong say goodbye to fans during the last night of their farewell concerts in Hong Kong February 1, 2005. Founded in 1983, "Beyond" lost its lead singer Koma Wong Ka-kui in an accident during a 1993 stage performance in Japan. [Reuters]
