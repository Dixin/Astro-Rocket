‘Because of You Ka Kui’ Officially Opens in Cinemas Nationwide

bythemagicrain
1 minute read
Total
0
Shares
0

Because of You Ka Kui (真的相识过), a documentary celebrating the life, music, ideals, and enduring influence of Chinese rock musician Wong Ka Kui, officially opened in cinemas nationwide from 11 June 2026. The wide release follows a successful fan screening and tribute concert that took place at The Exchange TRX on 6 June.

Produced by Yip Sai Wing and Leslie Chan, and directed by Rosa Pang, the documentary celebrates the life, music, ideals, and enduring influence of Wong Ka Kui, one of the most iconic figures in Chinese rock music. The film features exclusive interviews, rare archival materials, and heartfelt stories from musicians, friends, and industry figures, offering audiences an intimate look at Ka Kui’s legacy and the lasting impact of Beyond across generations.
